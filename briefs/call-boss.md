# Campaign Brief — Call Boss

## The Business

- **Product / service:** Call Boss — a live phone answering service and virtual assistant company for service-based local businesses (landscaping, lawn care, pest control, irrigation, tree service, HVAC, ELectrician, Plumbing, Garage Doors, Roofers, Pool installation, etc.), offering call answering, CRM management, estimate/scheduling support, and back-office work, including 24/7 coverage.
- **Primary goal of outreach:** Book a discovery/onboarding call ("Schedule A Call" via Calendly is the site's core CTA)
- **What counts as a win:** Meeting booked on the calendar (Calendly link) or a positive reply expressing interest

## The Offer

- **Core value proposition:** Never miss another customer call — Call Boss acts as a trained, always-available team that answers, converts leads, and runs CRM tasks so owners can focus on running the business instead of the phone.
- **Proof points available:** Multiple named customer testimonials (lawn care, landscaping, irrigation, pest control companies), direct CRM integration with Service Autopilot/Jobber/YardBook/CoPilot, woman-owned and minority-owned business, partnerships with industry tools (Deep Lawn, Kudos Marketing), founder story/podcast features
- **Call to action used in emails:** "Schedule a call" (Calendly link) — likely a short intro/onboarding call
- **Pricing / commitment level:** Tiered weekly plans — Standard ($127/wk, 30 min talk-time), Premium ($247/wk, 60 min + CRM access), Standard Plus ($257/wk, 90 min, 24/7), Premium Plus ($497/wk, 150 min, 24/7), Platinum ($597/wk, 250 min, 24/7), Back Office blocks at $99/hr; overage billed at $2.09/min

## Target Demographic

- **Industry / vertical:** Home/outdoor service businesses — confirmed by testimonials: landscaping, lawn care, pest control, irrigation, tree service, HVAC, ELectrician, Plumbing, Garage Doors, Roofers, Pool installation, Tree Service, etc.
- **Company size:** 50-100
- **Job titles targeted:** Likely Owner, Office Manager
- **Geography:** United States, Canada
- **Seniority / decision-making power:** Likely owners/decision-makers of small service businesses or Office Managers

## Audience Psychology

- **Top pain points this audience feels:**
  1. Missing incoming calls/leads because owner or staff is out in the field or short-staffed
  2. No budget or bandwidth to hire/train a full-time in-house receptionist
  3. Inconsistent customer service hurting reviews, retention, and growth
  4. Missed Calls leading to lost revenue
  5. Staffing and training headaches, high turnover in office
  6. No phone coverage outside of business hours or during times when team is otherwise unavailable.
  7. CRM and admin overloaded with tasks and taking calls at the same time
- **What they've probably already tried:** Answering calls themselves, using voicemail, hiring part-time admin/office staff, other answering services with limited capability (message-taking only)
- **Common objections we hear:** Too expensive, it makes more sense to bring it in house than hire you guys, they only want
- **How sophisticated is this audience about our category?** Likely knows the category (may have tried a basic answering service before) but may not know full-service/CRM-integrated options exist
- **Tone that resonates:** Warm, practical, relatable small-business-owner tone — testimonials are casual and personal, emphasizing relief/trust rather than corporate polish
- **common questions and answers:** Is your staff in the united states - Yes they are, can you get into our CRM system - Yes we can, do they speak english and sound clear - Yes they do, do I have to train them - Once and then from there we have ongoing training in your systems and processes, Why hire you when I can hire an office staff - We scale a lot better than hiring in house office staff and at a better cost.

- **How lists are verified/cleaned:** through Apollo
- **Sending domain(s) and warm-up status:** they are Warmed up

## History & Constraints

- **Previous campaigns to this audience and how they did:** not well. We haven't had anyone sign up yet from an email campaign.
- **Anything we must NOT say or claim:** Avoid overstating scope beyond stated services (don't claim sales/closing services beyond "outbound calls upon request"); don't misstate pricing — use tiered structure above; don't imply staff are full-time employees of the client's company. Please don't use em dashes in your copy.
- **Current hypotheses about what's working / not working:** Our messaging isn't matching with our target audience to where enough of them open the email.
