## The Business

- **Product / service:** Cappsure — a mobile app and cloud-based Field Operations Software platform that replaces paper-based reporting with GPS/geofence-verified mobile checklists, inspections, and work orders for multi-site facilities and field service teams.
- **Primary goal of outreach:** Book a demo call ("Schedule Demo" / "Book a demo" is the CTA on nearly every section of the site)
- **What counts as a win:** a positive reply showing interest or a booked demo call

## The Offer

- **Core value proposition:** Real-time, GPS-verified visibility and accountability across every site — property managers and facilities teams always know what work was done, where, and by whom, without chasing paper reports or disputing vendor billing.
- **Proof points available:** "7500+ Trusted By And Counting" client counter; Google Play/App Store reviews citing improved communication, billing accuracy, and revenue growth for landscape/property management users; positioned by third-party review site (Software Finder) as ideal for both small teams and large enterprises.
- **Call to action used in emails:** Worth a 15-min demo?, if that'd be useful I can send over a link? Book a quick 15 minute demo.
- **Pricing / commitment level:** Not published on the website (no pricing page/tiers shown). Third-party listing (Software Finder) notes pricing is available on request/varies by business needs — treat as a "custom quote" / sales-assisted pricing model.

## Target Demographic

- **Industry / vertical:** Multi-site facilities and property/vendor management — specifically: commercial property management, facilities management, municipalities & campuses, apartments, college campuses, commercial real estate, landscaping, snow removal, janitorial, street sweeping, parking & lighting, asphalt, roofing contractors, commercial painting contractors, general multi-site contractors, and other service businesses.
- **Company size:** 50 - 100
- **Job titles targeted:** Facilities managers, property managers/GMs, operations managers overseeing multiple sites, and the vendors/contractors who service those properties, Business Owners, .
- **Geography:** USA, Canada
- **Seniority / decision-making power:** Operations/facilities decision-makers responsible for vendor accountability, budget for maintenance/service contracts, and multi-site oversight (mid-management to director level implied by "facilities teams, property managers, service providers").

## Audience Psychology

- **Top 3 pain points this audience feels:**
  1. Lack of real-time visibility into whether vendors/field staff actually completed work on-site (leads to disputes and unplanned expenditures)
  2. Inconsistent, unprofessional, or hand-written reporting that's hard to track across many properties
  3. Labor overbilling / inability to verify time actually spent on-site
  4. inability to track assets exactly where they're located
  5. lack of information on when, where, and who tasks are performed.
  6. Unable to do accurate estimating and billing without enough details.
- **What they've probably already tried:** Manual/paper-based checklists and inspection reports, generic message-based communication (email/text) with vendors, possibly a general-purpose field service tool not built for multi-site property portfolios. Pictures and files randomly somewhere of where assets are located for their business
- **Common objections we hear:** Too expensive, Moving from one CRM to another is too difficult, lack of trust due to being unknown
- **How sophisticated is this audience about our category?** Likely moderately sophisticated — many will already use some field service/CRM tool (site references Zapier integration and comparisons to competitors), so messaging should differentiate on GPS/geofence verification and ease of adoption rather than educate from zero.
- **Tone that resonates:** Practical, accountability-and-efficiency focused, non-jargon-heavy; site emphasizes ease of use ("deploy in days"), reliability, and eliminating guesswork/paperwork rather than flashy tech-speak.
